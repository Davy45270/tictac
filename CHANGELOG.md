# Changelog

## 0.5.0

- “My other games” on the home screen: a short list of the author's three other games (Opencade, Opencadkid, Petit Astronome), each with its icon and one sentence, opened in the same tab.
- The list and its icons ship with the app, so the screen still shows offline; the network guard now allows these three plain links and nothing else.

## 0.4.0

- Four new lessons: answers written as digital times, a dial without numbers like a grown-up watch, a Roman-numeral dial, and “noon or midnight” where the sky tells the half of the day.
- Each lesson unlocks from the one it builds on, so the new dials open as soon as five-minute steps are known.
- Learning follow-up: a right answer on the first try counts towards mastery, a mistake brings a close exercise back later (in the session or at the start of the next one), and a burst of quick tries does not validate the stars.
- A lesson is marked mastered after two sessions with at least 18 first-try answers out of 20, no random tries and nothing left to review; a ribbon shows it on the home screen.
- Parents' area behind a small sum, with per-lesson progress, random tries and pending reviews. Everything stays on the device and can be erased; a test forbids any network channel in the app code.

## 0.3.0

- Illustrated look: a landscape that follows the time of day (morning, day, evening, night), glass panels and a toy alarm-clock dial with shaped, shadowed hands.
- Five redrawn guides with volume, light, fur and five distinct expressions; the chosen guide tints the interface.
- Rounded open-licence typeface bundled for offline play; golden result stars and gentle answer sparkles.
- Reduced motion stops every animation, including when the setting changes mid-game; contrast and size budgets checked by tests.

## 0.2.0

- Brighter result stars, a larger guide and clearer answer feedback.
- Improved small-screen layout and natural English time phrases.
- Regression coverage for raw drag angles, clockwise snapping ties and English addition clocks.

## 0.1.0

- Eight progressive clock-learning levels, French and English.
- Pure deterministic clock engine, forgiving retries and local progress.
- Touch and keyboard SVG clock, five original guides and offline PWA.
- Unit and browser test suites, continuous integration and Pages deployment.

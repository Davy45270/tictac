# Changelog

## 0.3.0

- Illustrated look: a landscape that follows the time of day (morning, day, evening, night), glass panels and a toy alarm-clock dial with shaped, shadowed hands.
- Five redrawn guides with volume, light, fur and five distinct expressions; the chosen guide tints the interface.
- Rounded open-licence typeface bundled for offline play; golden result stars and gentle answer sparkles.
- Reduced motion stops every animation, including when the setting changes mid-game; contrast and size budgets checked by tests.

## 0.2.0

- Brighter result stars, a larger guide and clearer answer feedback.
- Improved small-screen layout and natural English time phrases.
- Regression coverage for raw drag angles, clockwise snapping ties and English addition clocks.

## 0.1.0

- Eight progressive clock-learning levels, French and English.
- Pure deterministic clock engine, forgiving retries and local progress.
- Touch and keyboard SVG clock, five original guides and offline PWA.
- Unit and browser test suites, continuous integration and Pages deployment.
